# Privacy

AccessBridge AI stores language, accessibility preferences, saved plans, and recent chat history in the visitor's browser local storage. The frontend does not create an account or upload these saved plans to a project database.

When Live AI is enabled, the question and a limited recent conversation history are sent to the configured server endpoint for response generation. Do not enter passwords, one-time codes, financial details, government identifiers, medical records, or other sensitive personal information.

Important education, career, and service information must be verified with the relevant official provider before action is taken.
